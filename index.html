NOW SHOWING · 1980 – 2024

Hollywood:
A Closed Club?

Forty years of who works with whom, what the stories are about, and whether audiences notice.

DTU 02805 Social Graphs & Interactions · Final project · Fall 2026

THE PITCH

Research question

Over the last four decades, has Hollywood's collaboration network become more closed (more clustered, more repeat collaboration, more franchise-dominated), have its stories become more alike, and how does that relate to how audiences rate the films?

  • 01Is the network more clustered and more hub-dominated in the 2010s than in the 1980s, beyond what a degree-preserving random network would give?
  • 02Are plots within a decade, and within a collaboration community, more similar to each other over time?
  • 03Do films made inside tight circles rate differently from films that bring together people from different communities?

THE SCRIPT

Methods, in three acts

ACT I

The network closes

  • One collaboration network per decade
  • Clustering, degree distribution, hub share
  • Repeat-collaboration rate
  • Louvain communities and modularity
Check: the same measures on degree-preserving randomized networks.
ACT II

The stories converge

  • TF-IDF on TMDB plot overviews
  • Average plot similarity per decade and per community
  • Top words that characterize each community
  • Sentiment of plots over time
Check: hand-labelled sample of film pairs, similar or not, against the cosine score.
ACT III

Do audiences notice?

  • Rating vs. how embedded a film's people are
  • Bridge films (cross-community) vs. in-group films
  • Betweenness of directors vs. their films' ratings
Check: shuffle ratings across films and compare to the observed effect.
